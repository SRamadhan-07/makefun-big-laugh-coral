## Judge criteria summary (in priority order)

1. Genuinely hilarious — a real joke, not a quirky observation.
2. Unexpected twist — turns somewhere the reader did not see coming.
3. Tight — wastes no words; every word earns its place.
4. Fresh — not a recycled template, stock pun, or familiar joke.
5. Memorable — sticks in the head; a line someone repeats the same day.

The judge (pairwise, both orders, margin 1–5) is briefed as a stand-up comic
turned comedy editor. Wordplay, misdirection, and absurdly specific detail
count when they land; length for its own sake does not.
